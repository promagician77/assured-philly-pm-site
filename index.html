    Buy, Sell and Lease as tabs you still edit visually

    Squarespace 7.1 has no tabs block, so this is a short script for Code Injection, which your Core plan includes. It holds none of your content. Each tab is an ordinary Fluid Engine section, so text, photos and buttons are edited the normal way.

    Setup in your Squarespace account

    1. Give the three sections the anchor links buy, sell and lease (Edit Section, then Anchor Link).
    2. Optionally add a slim section above them with the anchor service-tabs. The tab bar sits inside it and takes its colours.
    3. Paste one block into Settings, Developer Tools, Code Injection, Footer.

    What it does

    • A link such as /services#lease opens that tab, so a Home page button can go straight to leasing.
    • Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs; screen readers hear real tabs.
    • While you edit in Squarespace, the tabs step aside and all three sections show.
    • With JavaScript off, the sections simply stack. Nothing is hidden by CSS alone.

    Email in three visits, because Google builds in two waits

    Google won't issue the DKIM key until Gmail has been on for 24 to 72 hours, and asks for 48 hours after SPF and DKIM before DMARC goes in. Rushed into one sitting, the setup would ship without the signature that ties your mail to assuredphillypm.com.

    1. Visit 1

      Verify the domain, point MX at Google, publish one SPF record, create contact@ and info@, and turn on "Send mail as" so replies leave from contact@.

    2. Visit 2, 24 to 72 hours after Gmail is on

      Generate a 2048-bit DKIM key in the Admin console, publish it at google._domainkey, then start authentication.

    3. Visit 3, 48 hours after SPF and DKIM

      Add DMARC in monitoring mode with a report address, then send a test to a personal Gmail and read "Show original": SPF, DKIM and DMARC must all say PASS.

    The records, in the order they go in
    VisitTypeHostValueCopy
    1MX@smtp.google.com priority 1; delete any other MX
    1TXT@v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
    2TXTgoogle._domainkeyv=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=… your 2048-bit key from the Admin console
    3TXT_dmarcv=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@assuredphillypm.com

    contact@ and info@. An alias delivers to one person's inbox. A Google Group lets several people see and answer the same mail. Which fits depends on who answers owner inquiries.

    One SPF record, always. When AppFolio, Buildium, a CRM or an email tool later sends as your domain, its entry joins the existing record. A second SPF record makes SPF fail for every message, and the checker above flags it.

    Four pages, planned around owners and renters

    Your logo, colours and copy go in as supplied. Every section also gets its own phone layout, which Fluid Engine keeps separate from desktop.

    Home
    One line for owners, one for renters, and two buttons: owner inquiry and current rentals. Service summaries link straight into the matching tab.
    Services
    Intro, the tab bar, then Buy, Sell and Lease as three editable sections.
    Current Rentals
    One item per unit (photos, rent, beds and baths, available date, request a showing) so new units appear automatically. Later this section can switch to your platform's listings feed, so units are entered once. A link to your written tenant screening criteria sits on the page: Philadelphia's Renters' Access Act requires landlords to provide them before taking an application or fee.
    Contact and owner inquiry
    An owner form (property, units, occupancy, what you need) that emails contact@ and copies each inquiry to a Google Sheet as a backup. Zapier storage can feed a CRM later.

    Footer on every page: your licensed brokerage name and office phone (Pennsylvania's advertising rule, 49 Pa. Code § 35.305) and the Equal Housing Opportunity notice. I'm not a lawyer; the wording should be confirmed with your broker of record.
